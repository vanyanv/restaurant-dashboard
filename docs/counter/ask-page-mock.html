LayoutRail · thread · dock

216px conversations rail beside the 212px app rail, a 760px reading column, and a docked composer that never scrolls away. The page head is the conversation's title, so the headline is the answer. Store and range travel in the URL and are shown once, above the composer.

StoppingSend becomes Stop; a stopped turn is kept

While a turn runs the send button morphs into a stop square (Esc does the same). Stopping keeps whatever streamed, marks the turn "Stopped", and offers Continue — the question is never thrown away. Today use-ask.ts has no stop(); the AI SDK's useChat provides one.

Older conversationsGrouped by day, searched by title and text

Today · Yesterday · This week · then months, with the current thread marked. Search (/ from the rail) filters titles and turn text — the ?cq= search that already exists. Saved questions pin to the top with a clock.

Rename · delete⋯ on a thread, or the head of the open one

Rename is inline (Enter saves, Esc keeps the old name). Delete arms in place — "Delete for good? / Keep" — the two-step that shipped in thread-actions.tsx, now also reachable from the rail. Delete-all arms the same way. On the phone, swipe a row left.

ThreadTurns, day separators, and a way back down

Each turn is question → answer block → footer. A day separator marks where a thread crossed midnight. Scroll up during a turn and a "New answer" pill appears; it never yanks you to the bottom.

PhoneSame thread, rail becomes a sheet

"Threads" in the head opens a bottom sheet (iOS drawer curve, 260ms) with the same list, search and swipe-to-delete. The composer docks above the tabs with the mic; the hints row and scope note are dropped for room.
