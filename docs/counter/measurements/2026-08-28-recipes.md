# Recipes, measured before it was built

`P.recipes` (`docs/counter/counter-prototype.html:6107`) and `P.recipe`
(`:6151`) against the live account, 2026-08-28. **60 recipes, 129 lines, 41
sellable, 26 confirmed.**

The prototype's fixture describes a different restaurant. Four of its landmarks
have no data behind them here, and the two things that ARE wrong with this
account's recipes are not among the things it looks for.

---

## 1. Nothing is AI-generated, so the prototype's second cell is empty

`Recipe.isAiGenerated` is **false on all 60 rows**. There is not one
AI-generated recipe in the account.

The prototype gives its third strip cell to `AI-generated, unconfirmed · 9 ·
waiting on you` and leads its queue with *"Nine AI-generated recipes are
unconfirmed — they cost out and they feed COGS, but nobody has checked the
quantities."* Both are about a population of size zero.

The `RecipeMappingProposal` table is where the AI proposals actually live, and
`2026-08-28-ingredients.md` §4 already measured it: ten rows, all ten decided,
nothing pending. So the AI angle is genuinely closed on both tables and the
cell has to be about something else.

**What is waiting is 34 unconfirmed recipes that a person typed**, and the
volume behind them is not small — §2.

## 2. Unconfirmed is 34 of 60, and it carries $536,329 of revenue

| | recipes | sold, 90d | revenue, 90d |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| confirmed | 20 | 181,944 | $1,969,299 |
| **unconfirmed** | **21** | **74,826** | **$536,329** |

*(sellable recipes only; 19 of the 60 are modifiers and never sell on their own.)*

**21.4% of ninety days' revenue is priced by a recipe nobody has confirmed.**
Led by Coca Cola 20oz (19,308 sold), the Signature Slider Fries & Drink Combo
(17,916) and Side of Yellow Chilies (15,051).

Confirmation is not decoration here: these recipes feed `DailyCogsItem`, which
feeds the COGS page, the menu-profit margins and the P&L food line. The
prototype's `Confirmed 114 · 89.1%` is a reassuring number. The honest one is
**26 of 60 · 43.3%**, and the cell should say what is riding on the other half.

## 3. Nothing is "uncosted" — and two plates cost nothing, which is worse

The prototype's fourth cell is `Uncosted · 5 · missing an ingredient price`,
and its second queue item is *"Five recipes cannot be costed. Each has at least
one ingredient with no matched price."*

Walking all 60 recipes through `computeRecipeCost`:

```
recipes                 60
partial                  0
errored                  0
lines                  129   (91 ingredient, 38 sub-recipe)
lines with no canonical  0
lines matched, no price  0
```

**Every line in every recipe prices.** The cell is zero.

What is actually wrong is one layer up. **Eight recipes have no lines at all**,
and they do not read as uncosted — they read as costed, at whatever
`foodCostOverride` says:

| recipe | override | lines | sold 90d | revenue 90d |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| The Reverse Bun | **$0.00** | 0 | 546 | **$4,324.32** |
| Minute Maid (20 oz cup) | $0.30 | 0 | 438 | $1,547.22 |
| Mod: Loaded Plain (Cheese & Sauce Only) | **−$0.07** | 0 | — | — |
| Mod: Make Halal | $0.00 | 0 | — | — |
| Mod: Meat and Cheese | $0.00 | 0 | — | — |
| Mod: No Salt | $0.00 | 0 | — | — |
| Mod: Remove Cheese | $0.00 | 0 | — | — |
| Mod: Reverse Bun | $0.00 | 0 | — | — |

**The Reverse Bun is a slider that sells for $7.92 and declares a food cost of
zero.** It is `isConfirmed = true` — somebody signed it off. It has appeared in
`DailyCogsItem` on **195 days, and every one of those 195 rows is flagged
`partialCost`**: the cost walk has been saying "this total is an
understatement" every day since 31 May and nothing surfaces it. Its recorded
cost across those days is $93.39 against $4,324 of revenue — **2.2% food
cost**, for a burger.

A zero override and no override are different intentions stored as
indistinguishable values, and one of them silently books 100% margin. That is
the same family as invoice G95788-00 in `2026-08-28-invoices.md` §2: a header
with nothing under it, passing every check because the check needs lines to
have an opinion.

`Mod: Loaded Plain` goes one further and carries a **negative** food cost.

## 4. Every recipe yields 1, so the Yield column is a column of ones

`Recipe.servingSize` is `1` on **all 60 rows**.

The prototype's table carries a `Yield` column and its component section is
built on batch yields — `House sauce · 96 oz · $0.22 / oz`, `Slider seasoning ·
32 oz · $0.14 / oz`. That is a real and useful shape and this account does not
have it: there is no batch recipe with a yield, so there is no cost-per-ounce
to divide out.

A `Yield` column of sixty 1s is a column that costs a reader a glance and
returns nothing. Dropped; the cost is per serving and per serving is per one.

## 5. Component recipes are real, and bigger than the prototype's three

38 of the 129 lines point at another recipe. **15 recipes are used inside
others:**

| component | used in |
|---|---:|
| Straight Cut Fries | 8 |
| Extra Chris N Eddy's Sauce | 6 |
| Mod: Add Grilled Onion | 5 |
| Double Slider | 4 |
| Mod: Add Sauce | 3 |
| Mod: Add Tomato | 2 |
| Mod: Add Lettuce | 2 |
| *(8 more, used in 1 each)* | 1 |

This is the one section of the prototype that lands as drawn, with two
differences: there are 15 rather than 3, and `Double Slider` is both a
top-selling plate (242,865 sold, $11.59) and a component of four other
recipes. A "component recipes" table that lists it next to Straight Cut Fries
without saying so invites the reader to think it is a prep item.

## 6. The catalogue is frozen, exactly like the ingredient catalogue

```
first created   2026-02-27
last created    2026-04-27
last updated    2026-05-03
```

**No recipe has been created in four months and none edited in almost four.**
The prototype's first cell reads `Recipes · 128 · +6 this month`; the honest
delta is `none added in 30 days`, which is the same sentence the Ingredients
page prints for the same reason (`2026-08-28-ingredients.md` §1). Two
catalogues that stopped being maintained on the same fortnight in early May.

## 7. What the page can price, and what it cannot

35 of the 41 sellable recipes have a non-zero POS price via `OtterItemMapping`
(180 mappings, 38 recipes) over the last 90 days, so `Cost / serving`,
`Sells at` and `Margin` are all computable for 35 rows.

The other 6 have no mapped POS item and no price. They get an em-dash, not a
zero — a recipe with no observed sale has no margin, and printing 100% or 0%
would be inventing one.

Modifier recipes are excluded from margin entirely: they are never sold on
their own, their POS sub-item price is null in almost every case, and a margin
column against a null price is a column of noise.

---

## What this changes about the build

1. **Strip:** `Recipes 60 · none added in 30 days` · `Confirmed 26 · 43.3%` ·
   `Unconfirmed revenue $536,329 · 21.4% of 90d` · `Cost $0.00 · 2 plates`.
   The AI cell is gone; nothing is AI-generated.
2. **The queue leads with The Reverse Bun**, not with unconfirmed counts — a
   sellable plate at $0.00 food cost, flagged partial on 195 consecutive days,
   is worth more than a tally.
3. **No `Yield` column.** Every recipe yields 1.
4. **Component recipes keep their section** and gain a marker for the two that
   are also sold on their own.
5. **Margin is em-dashed, never zeroed,** for the 6 sellable recipes with no
   observed price.
