# Menu catalog — measured before anything is built

Probed 2026-08-28, trailing 30 days, all three stores.

`P.catalog` (`docs/counter/counter-prototype.html:6059`) is "the POS menu as it
sells, and whether each item has a recipe behind it". Every figure it asks for
is answerable. Three of them answer differently enough to change the page.

## The strip, measured

| prototype | here |
|---|---:|
| Items 84 · 9 categories | **61 · 8 categories** |
| Mapped to a recipe 78 · 92.9% | **54 · 88.5%** |
| Unmapped 6 · blocking COGS on $1,240 | **7 · $62** |
| Modifiers 41 · 36 costed | **54 · 39 costed** |

## 1. The unmapped items are POS junk, and the call to action is nearly empty

Seven items carry no recipe. Six of them sold **once each, for about three
dollars**: `loadfries 24`, `load 602`, `load 533`, `loaded on fries`,
`Open item #1`, `add patty to fries` — open-item rows and typos, not menu
items. The seventh is a **$48 baseball cap** (`Chris N Eddy's Ball-Cap
(Limited Run)`), which is merchandise and has no recipe by nature.

Sixty-two dollars, against the prototype's $1,240. "Map them" would send the
owner to write recipes for six typos and a hat.

## 2. The real mapping gap is modifiers, and it is invisible to a revenue report

Fifteen of fifty-four modifiers are unmapped, carrying **$213** of revenue —
which sounds smaller still, until the quantities:

| unmapped modifier | sold | revenue |
|---|---:|---:|
| Add Pickles | 2,250 | **$0** |
| Add Sauce | 919 | **$0** |
| Add Raw Onions | 215 | **$0** |
| Add Grilled Onions | 190 | **$0** |
| Remove Cheese | 159 | $0 |
| Make it a Triple | 82 | $139 |

**Over 3,700 servings of food left the kitchen with no cost against them**,
because a free modifier earns nothing and so never appears in anything ranked
by revenue. That is the gap on this menu, and it is the one the prototype's
"Unmapped items" queue is shaped to carry.

## 3. THE CATEGORY RING WOULD LIE, AND THAT IS THE PAGE'S BEST FINDING

The prototype draws "By category · sales share in this range". Measured:

| category | items | revenue | share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| On The Side | 10 | $106,750 | **41.5%** |
| NFL Promo | **1** | $50,911 | 19.8% |
| Combos | 4 | $46,875 | 18.2% |
| Slider and Fries Combos | 4 | $24,118 | 9.4% |
| Drinks | 23 | $14,743 | 5.7% |
| Secret Menu | 5 | $6,952 | 2.7% |
| A La Carte | 10 | $6,516 | 2.5% |
| Uncategorized | 11 | $95 | 0.0% |

Read as drawn, that ring says **41.5% of this restaurant's sales are side
dishes**. They are not. `On The Side` holds `Signature Double Patty & Cheese
Slider (Chris' or Eddy's Way)` — 6,913 sold, the single biggest seller on the
menu — along with `Triple Patty Slider` and `Chris N Eddy's Slider`. And
`NFL Promo`, a *promotion*, is one item holding a fifth of all revenue.

Two more: `Loaded Fries` appears under both `On The Side` and `A La Carte`,
and eleven items have no category at all.

The POS categories do not describe the food. A ring drawn from them is a
picture of the POS's filing, not of what the restaurant sells — and an owner
reading it would conclude something false about their own menu. So the section
keeps its slot and reports **the state of the categories themselves**: which
category holds what, how much revenue each carries, and the fact that the
largest one is misnamed. That is a finding the owner can act on; the share of
sales by category is not, until the filing is fixed.

Fourth time in this rebuild a prototype section has had to change its subject.

## 4. The AI-proposal card has nothing to say

`RecipeMappingProposal` holds **7 REJECTED and 3 ACCEPTED. Zero pending.** The
prototype's "Five AI mapping proposals are waiting … Review the nine
unconfirmed" has no counterpart. The queue carries the modifier gap instead.

## What the table can show

Every column the prototype asks for exists. Item and category from
`OtterMenuItem`; Price as revenue over quantity (`fpTotalSales + tpTotalSales`
over `fpQuantitySold + tpQuantitySold`); Sold as that quantity; Recipe from
whether `OtterItemMapping` carries the name; Plate cost and Margin from
`DailyCogsItem` (`lineCost / qtySold`, and `1 - lineCost / salesRevenue`).
