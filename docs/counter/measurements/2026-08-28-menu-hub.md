# Menu hub — measured before anything is built

Probed 2026-08-28 against the live database. Item counts are over the trailing
30 days of `OtterMenuItem`, which is what "the menu" means here: what the POS
actually sold, not a catalogue somebody maintains.

## The four strip cells

| cell | prototype | measured | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Items | 84 · 9 categories | **61 · 8 categories** | distinct non-modifier `itemName` |
| Blended margin | 68.6% | **78.1%** | `DailyCogsItem`, cost over menu revenue |
| Unmapped | 6 | **7 of 61** | sold with no `OtterItemMapping` |
| Uncosted modifiers | 5 of 41 | **15 of 54** | sold with no `OtterSubItemMapping` |

Every one is answerable. That is unusual for this rebuild — Labor lost two
cells and COGS lost two — and it is because this page asks about the menu's
own shape rather than about money crossing a denominator.

## The margin is on MENU revenue, and it will not match COGS

**78.1%** is cost over `DailyCogsItem.salesRevenue`. The COGS page's 28.4%
food cost is over the statement's Total Sales, which implies a 71.6% margin —
**seven and a half points apart, and both correct.**

They answer different questions. A menu page asks what an item is worth when
it sells: that is its own revenue, item by item, blended. A cost page asks
what the food line took out of the business: that is Total Sales, because
Total Sales is what the P&L divides by.

This is the same split the COGS items table already states on the page —
*"these rows do not sum to the figure above, and are not meant to"* — and the
Menu hub must say it too rather than leave a reader to find it.

## What the donut shows, and the thing it exposes

Items per category, 30 days. The prototype's centre is the item count (`'84'`),
not a dollar figure, so the ring counts items rather than money:

| category | items |
|---|---:|
| Drinks | 23 |
| **Uncategorized** | **11** |
| On The Side | 10 |
| A La Carte | 10 |
| Secret Menu | 5 |
| Combos | 4 |
| Slider and Fries Combos | 4 |
| NFL Promo | 1 |

**"Uncategorized" is the second-largest category on the menu.** Eleven of
sixty-one items carry no category at all. That is a real finding about this
account's menu data and the page should show it as its own slice rather than
folding it into an "Other" wedge — a ring that hides its own biggest data gap
is decoration.

Note also that a ring by ITEM COUNT and a ring by COST are different pictures:
Drinks is 23 of 61 items here and 7.1% of cost on the COGS page. Both rings are
honest; neither is the other.

## Coverage behind the page

`OtterMenuItem` 22,506 rows · `OtterItemMapping` 180 · `OtterSubItemMapping`
150 · `Recipe` 60. Of 54 modifiers sold in the window, 15 have no mapping and
**0 are mapped to a null recipe** — so the gap is entirely "never mapped",
not "mapped badly", which is a different fix and worth saying.
