# Vendors, measured before it was built

`P.vendors` (`docs/counter/counter-prototype.html`, the `P.vendors` block)
against the live account, 2026-08-28. 226 invoices, $383,937 all-time, **10
vendor strings covering 6 vendors**.

`/dashboard/vendors` — the rail's own destination until this session — has
never existed; the working page is the pre-Counter one at
`/dashboard/operations/vendors`, and `/dashboard/operations/vendors/{id}` is
still a 404.

---

## 1. Half the strip has no data, and it is the half about time

**`Median lead time · 2.4 days · ▼ 0.3`** and **`On time · 92% · ▲ 4 pts`**.

`VendorLeadTime` holds **0 rows**. The table exists, `@@unique([accountId,
vendorNameNormalized])` and all, and `/api/cron/vendor-lead-time` exists to
fill it, and it is empty. There is no lead time for any vendor.

"On time" is worse, because it is not a backfill away: being on time needs a
date the delivery was PROMISED for, and no table in this schema carries one.
`Invoice` has `invoiceDate`, `dueDate` and `emailReceivedAt` — when it was
billed, when payment is owed, when the mail arrived. None of them is a promise.

**What is computable is delivery CADENCE**, and it is not the same thing:

| vendor | deliveries | median gap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sysco | 88 | 2.0 days |
| Premier Meats & Crystal Bay | 53 | 2.5 days |
| Individual FoodService | 52 | 3.0 days |
| Vitco Foodservice | 24 | 3.0 days |

Lead time is order to delivery. This is delivery to delivery. They answer
different questions and the page must not print one under the other's label —
so the cell is `Delivers every`, and the two cells the prototype wanted are
replaced by ones this account can answer.

## 2. Ten strings, six vendors — and the merge is provable here

| `vendorName` | invoices | spend | window |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Premier Meats & Crystal Bay | 53 | $114,412 | Mar 4 – Aug 27 |
| Sysco | 66 | $104,038 | Feb 23 – Aug 27 |
| Individual FoodService | 52 | $52,216 | Feb 23 – Aug 27 |
| **Sysco Los Angeles, Inc.** | 24 | $51,392 | Feb 19 – **Apr 30** |
| Vitco Foodservice | 13 | $25,149 | Apr 23 – Aug 27 |
| **VITCO FOODSERVICE** | 13 | $21,903 | Jun 1 – Jul 25 |
| **Premier Meats** | 2 | $7,230 | Apr 9 – Apr 13 |
| Bear State Kitchen | 1 | $3,398 | Jan 15 |
| Premier Deli Services, Inc. | 1 | $3,032 | Apr 8 |
| **IFS INDIVIDUAL FOODSERVICE** | 1 | $1,166 | Jul 1 |

The date windows are the evidence. `Sysco Los Angeles, Inc.` **stops on 30
April** and `Sysco` runs through August — one supplier whose invoice template
changed, not two suppliers one of which vanished. `Premier Meats` occupies four
days inside `Premier Meats & Crystal Bay`'s six-month run.

Merged, this account buys from **6 vendors**, and Sysco is **$155,430** rather
than the $104,038 a `GROUP BY vendorName` reports. That figure is the one the
Invoices page already prints; this page is where it is a row.

`Bear State Kitchen` and `Premier Deli Services, Inc.` stay separate. One
invoice each, and sharing a word with Premier Meats is not evidence of sharing
a legal entity.

## 3. `Reconciles` is the column with teeth, and it already has an answer

The prototype's last column is a `Clean` / problem badge. This one is real, and
it is the invoice work from earlier this session: summing goods lines against
the printed subtotal, with the four delivery-charge rows excluded.

| vendor | invoices | not reconciling | in review |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Individual FoodService | 53 | **3** | 5 |
| Sysco | 90 | **3** | 3 |
| Premier Meats & Crystal Bay | 55 | **1** | 1 |
| Vitco Foodservice | 26 | 0 | 9 |
| the other three | 3 | 0 | 0 |

Seven invoices across three vendors. Nothing here is a vendor-quality
judgement — every one of the seven is an extraction defect on our side, not a
billing error on theirs — so the column reports what our records do, and says
so.

## 4. Price trend has to be indexed, not compared

Basket medians over eight weeks:

```
Sysco                        $35.61 – $40.99   (per case)
Individual FoodService       $33.41 – $43.01   (per case)
Vitco Foodservice            $87.71 – $118.71  (per case)
Premier Meats & Crystal Bay  $ 4.33 – $  4.61  (per pound)
```

These are not comparable numbers. Premier Meats bills by the pound and Vitco by
the case, so a shared axis puts the largest vendor in the account on the floor
of the chart. Same problem the Ingredients price monitor solved: **each vendor
is indexed to its own first week and the axis is percent.**

---

## What this changes about the build

1. **`Median lead time` and `On time` are replaced.** No lead-time rows exist
   and no promise date exists in the schema. The cells become `Delivers every`
   (median gap, which IS computable and is named for what it measures) and
   `Does not reconcile`.
2. **Vendors are merged before anything is counted** — 6, not 10, and Sysco at
   $155,430.
3. **The spend chart is indexed percent, not dollars per unit**, or the biggest
   vendor draws as a flat line.
4. **`Reconciles` says whose defect it is.** Ours.
